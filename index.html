Meet the founder

Dr. Peter Esperanza — Math Professor & Online Educator

Dr. E built his flipped math classroom at Barstow High School, starting in 2012 — along with the research behind it and, eventually, the YouTube channel. After 14 years at the high school, he brought the model to Barstow Community College, where he teaches today.

He founded Numberbender on a simple belief: great math instruction should be free and available to anyone — student or teacher, anywhere in the world. His video lessons have been viewed more than 16 million times.
