Who made this

Dr. Peter Esperanza taught AP Calculus and AP Statistics at Barstow High School in California, and flipped both classes in 2012. In his first year teaching AP Statistics, exactly one of his students passed the AP exam; after the flip, about ten a year passed. He now teaches math at Barstow Community College, and his lessons have reached students around the world through the Numberbender YouTube channel. More about Dr. E →
