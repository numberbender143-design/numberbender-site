Start here

Up and running in about three minutes

You do not need a capture card, a green screen, or OBS. If you can open a browser tab and share your screen, you already know how to do this.

1

Open Studio

Go to numberbender.com/studio in Chrome or Edge. No account, no download. Click Allow when your browser asks for the camera.

2

Pick a layout, add your second pane

Choose Side-by-side, Spotlight, or Dual. Then add what goes next to you — your slides, your iPad screen, or a second app window.

3

Share the Studio tab

In Zoom, Meet, or Teams, hit Share Screen and pick the Numberbender Studio tab. Your students see the finished broadcast, not your messy desktop.

💡 Tip: tick Share tab audio in Zoom or Meet if your lesson includes video or sound.

The tutorials

Five setups, five short videos

Each one is a single setup you can copy in your next class. Start with the first if you teach live on Zoom; start with the second if you are building a following on Reels or Shorts.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is Numberbender Studio really free?

Yes. No login, no trial, no paywall. Numberbender is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and Studio is part of that mission — good teaching should not be locked behind a subscription.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Studio runs entirely in your browser. If you want it in your dock or taskbar, you can optionally install it as an app from your browser's address bar on Mac or PC.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, and other Chromium browsers on Windows, macOS, and ChromeOS. Safari and Firefox have limited screen-capture support, so Chrome or Edge is recommended.

Does it work with Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams?

Yes. Studio does not replace your meeting app — you screen-share the Studio browser tab into whatever platform you already use. That is why it works everywhere.

Does it work on a Chromebook?

Yes. Studio runs in Chrome on ChromeOS, which is one reason it is popular with public school teachers working on issued devices.

Can I record my lesson?

Yes. Studio can record your composed layout directly, including vertical formats for Reels, Shorts, and TikTok. You can also just record through Zoom if that is already your workflow.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Your camera and screen stay on your own device. Studio composes the layout locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded to Numberbender.

Puwede ba itong gamitin sa DepEd online classes?

Opo. Libre po ito at gumagana sa kahit anong laptop na may Chrome. Marami pong Filipino teachers ang gumagamit nito para sa online at hybrid classes — walang bayad, walang login.
