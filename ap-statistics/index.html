Where Numberbender started: an AP Statistics classroom

Dr. Peter Esperanza began teaching AP Statistics at Barstow High School in 2009. In 2012 he flipped the class — short video lessons the night before, problems together in class the next day — and those videos became Numberbender.

The year before the flip, exactly one of his students passed the AP exam. Once the class was flipped, about ten students a year were passing, with scores of 3, 4 and 5. The lessons on this page come from that classroom.
